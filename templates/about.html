About Path2Learn

Path2Learn is built to make school discovery easier, clearer, and more practical for students and parents in Rwanda.

Mission

I aim to provide a clear, reliable, and easy-to-use database of schools across Rwanda so families can make confident educational decisions.

Vision

I envision a future where every Rwandan student can discover a school that fits their potential, and every parent can make informed choices with confidence.

Built by Moise IRANEJEJE

Path2Learn is my personal flagship project, built to use technology for social impact in Rwanda. This platform is my contribution to that vision.
