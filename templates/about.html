About Path2Learn

A dedicated platform designed to bridge the information gap for students and parents in Rwanda, helping them discover the perfect educational institutions.

Our Mission

To provide a comprehensive, easy-to-use, and reliable database of schools across Rwanda, empowering users with the information they need to make informed decisions about education.

Our Vision

A future where every Rwandan student can easily find a school that matches their potential, and every parent can confidently support their child's educational journey.

Developed by Tech Inspires Organisation

Path2Learn is a flagship project from a group of visionaries committed to leveraging technology for social good in Rwanda. This platform is our contribution to that vision.
